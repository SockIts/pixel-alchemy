How to play

Begin with Earth, Water, Wind and Fire. Select any two elements from the left inventory, or drag them onto the two slots of the mixing table.

Hit MIX ELEMENTS. The two sprites fly together and react at the center. If the recipe works, the new element appears in your inventory and unlocks a card in the catalog below.

Try Fire + Water to make Steam, or Fire + Earth to make Lava. You can combine an element with itself, too.

Every elemental spirit is rendered with code: crisp pixel particles, spell rings, glow and animated magical effects. No image assets.
